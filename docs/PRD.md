# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# UD-AI (Understanding Data AI)

## Tagline
**Universal Knowledge Understanding Platform**

## Elevator Pitch
UD-AI is an AI platform that accepts information from documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, and other knowledge sources, builds a Unified Knowledge Model (UKM), and generates visual, structured, explainable, and interactive knowledge artifacts that improve learning and decision-making.

---

# 1. Vision
**Vision:** Transform information into understanding.

**Mission:** Enable anyone to understand complex information in minutes instead of hours.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Current document AI systems primarily summarize text. They generally do not:
- Understand relationships between concepts
- Integrate text, tables, charts, formulas, and diagrams into one coherent explanation
- Produce visual learning artifacts
- Adapt explanations to different users
- Provide evidence-backed, explainable outputs

UD-AI addresses these limitations by acting as a Universal Knowledge Understanding Platform.
